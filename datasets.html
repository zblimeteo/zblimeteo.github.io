ERA5 Northern Hemisphere Extratropical and Arctic Cyclone System Tracks, 1940–2024

Version 1.0.0 · 1,020 monthly CSV files · CC BY 4.0

ERA5-derived cyclone system tracks at 6-hourly UTC times, with cyclone-center coordinates, sea-level pressure, size and motion diagnostics, and track and event identifiers. The archive supports the analysis of all-season cyclone activity over the Arctic Ocean, North Atlantic and North Pacific. The Zenodo record includes a README with field definitions and guidance on reading tracks that span month boundaries.
