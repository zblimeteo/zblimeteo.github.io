Climate scientist

Zhi-Bo Li

Climate dynamics · Extreme climate · Wind and energy · Paleoclimate

I am a climate scientist studying the connections between near-surface winds, sea-level change and extreme weather. I also investigate deep-time paleoclimate, global monsoons, atmospheric teleconnections and aridity using observations and climate-model simulations. International collaboration is always welcome.
